Junior Technical Analyst (AI & Data Engineering)
Paradigm Capital Group — Toronto, ON
Source: Indeed JOBSEARCH_100022, posted 2026-09-16

We are not a traditional firm running on bloated slide decks and manual spreadsheets. We are a tech-native M&A firm replacing old-school deal sourcing with custom LLM pipelines, predictive market mapping, and automated outreach engines.

We’re hiring a Junior Technical Analyst to help engineer the data backbone of our origination platform. You'll work directly at the intersection of high finance, data pipelines, and applied AI.

*What You’ll Actually Do*

* *Build & Tune Data Engines:* Scrape, clean, and structure unstructured B2B market data to feed our deal-sourcing algorithms.
* *Refine Model Logic:* Optimize prompt frameworks and custom LLM API integrations (OpenAI, Anthropic) to ensure our outreach hits with precision and high context.
* *Map Emerging Markets:* Run SQL and Python workflows to identify off-market, high-value acquisition targets before anyone else sees them.
* *Automate Grunt Work:* Continuously spot operational bottlenecks and build scripts or webhooks to eliminate them.

*What Gets You the Role*

* Strong foundation in Python, SQL, and working with modern API architectures.
* A genuine curiosity for tech, business models, and how software companies scale.
* 1–3 years of hands-on experience (internships, bootcamps, or heavy personal projects count).
* *Tangible proof of work:* We care far more about what you can build than where you went to school. A link to your GitHub, a live side project, or a technical write-up where you breakdown a project carries massive weight with us.

*Why Join Us*

* *Zero Bureaucracy:* If a custom script can save 10 hours of manual research, you get the green light to build and deploy it immediately.
* *Real Leverage:* You won't be formatting pitch books—you will be building systems that drive real enterprise transactions.
* *High-Signal Environment:* Work alongside a lean, fast-moving team that values execution over face time.

*How to Apply* Skip the templated cover letter—we won't read it. Send your resume along with a link to a project, repo, or article you're proud of, and a brief note on a data automation tool or model you’ve experimented with recently.

All the best.
